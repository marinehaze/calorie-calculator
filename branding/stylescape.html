Calorie & Nutrition App — Mobile

Stage 02 / Branding · Locked direction · Round 03

One direction, locked. Three ways to make it recognisable.

Table is the foundation: warm paper, one berry accent, editorial hierarchy. What it gains here is the calm card structure of a good modern health app, lighter typography, and food photography promoted from supporting evidence to a first-class part of the brand. Everything below is one direction — no alternatives. The last section holds the only open question: three candidate brand devices, all built inside this same direction.

What changed from Round 02

Cards adopted. “Nothing sits in a box” gave calm but no grouping. Content now groups into white cards on paper, with hairlines separating rows inside them.
One type family. DM Sans dropped. Instrument Sans carries display, body and data; monospace is a 10 px detail.
Lighter figures. Hero numbers set at 400, not 600 — presence comes from scale and the space around them.
Softer icons. Squared terminals replaced with rounded, at a lighter 1.5 px.
Accent retuned. Bramble warmed and saturated a step so it holds its own beside real food.
Photography promoted. From “once per surface” to a first-class element with its own crop rules.

01 — Colour

Warm paper, one berry, and food doing the rest

Bramble is a food colour — fig, plum, radicchio, beetroot — which is why it sits comfortably beside a photograph without looking like an interface swatch dropped on top of one.

It is deliberately not green. Every app in this category reaches for green, and green also competes directly with the herbs and vegetables in the imagery. Most food photography lives in oranges, browns and greens; a berry accent stays clear of that range, so the accent reads as the product’s voice and the food reads as food.

The accent never covers more than about a tenth of a screen. It is a mark, not a surface.
It has exactly three jobs: the current value, the active state, and the single primary action. Nothing else earns it.
Macros are three steps of one hue, never three different colours. Ranking is read as density, not as a colour key.
No red / amber / green semantics. A value over target is stated in words; nothing turns red and scolds the user.
Photography is the second colour. It is allowed to be the loudest thing on the screen — the palette is built quiet so it can be.

02 — Typography

One voice, working from 10 px to 128 px

Instrument Sans alone. A refined contemporary grotesk with slightly narrow proportions and a faint editorial warmth — enough character to not read as a system default, restrained enough to disappear behind the numbers.

Large figures are set at 400, never heavier. Presence comes from scale, tight tracking and the space kept clear around them. Every figure in the product is tabular, so values stay in column while they recalculate.

03 — Layout principles

Paper holds cards. Cards hold rows.

Grouping happens once, at the card. Inside it, hairlines separate rows; outside it, only space does. That single rule is what keeps a data-heavy screen from turning into a dashboard.

One hero number per screen. Everything else is a value.
Hairlines only inside cards. Between cards, space does the separating.
8 px base. 24 px card padding, 16 px between cards, 40 px between groups.
Images bleed to the card edge. Never inset on all four sides — that reads as a banner.
One left rail. Titles, body and labels share an edge; values align right against it.
Radius 16 on cards, 10 on media and inputs, 99 only on the progress track.
The accent appears at most three times on any one screen.

05 — Photography

Real food, honestly lit

One broad soft daylight source with gentle directional falloff. Shot three-quarter or straight on; overhead only when the dish is round. The food fills the frame and the surface behind it stays quiet — no linen, no scattered crumbs, no props narrating a lifestyle.

Not the overhead flat-lay grid (reads as stock), not moody restaurant photography with a black background (reads as a menu), not a glossy hero on a coloured banner (reads as delivery). Warm but true colour, no heavy grading. Food should look like something you could actually make.

06 — Icons

1.5 px, rounded, no fills

Drawn on a 24 px grid at a constant 1.5 px stroke with rounded caps and joins — light enough to sit beside 400-weight type without out-weighing it. Outline only; the active state changes colour to the accent rather than filling the shape.

08 — The open question

Three candidate brand devices

One recurring visual rule, applied everywhere, is what separates a competent light interface from a recognisable product. All three below live inside the direction above — same paper, same accent, same type — and each one is a different kind of rule: a marker, an image relationship, and a structural device.

Each is shown at the two or three sizes it would actually have to survive. Pick one. Two of them can coexist only by weakening both.

Device 01 · Nutrition marker

The measure

Every key number carries a short rule beneath it, and the rule’s length is that value as a share of its target. The figure and its scale become a single object, so a number is never shown without its context.

Device 02 · Image + number

The hanging plate

Food is always cropped to a circle, and the circle hangs past the left edge of its card by a quarter of its diameter. The calorie figure sits on the circle’s centre line. The plate breaks the grid; the number never breaks the plate.

What is locked. Palette, type system, layout principles, nutrition hierarchy, photography direction, crop rules and icon construction. These are settled unless you want to reopen them.

What is open. The brand device, and only that. Section 08 holds three candidates and no recommendation — the choice changes what the product looks like in a screenshot more than any other decision left in this stage.

On the photography. Every image here is a tone and light study rather than a photograph — outbound image sources are unreachable from this environment. They carry the intended grade, contrast and crop, not a subject. Real imagery is the first thing to test once a device is chosen, and it matters most for device 02.
