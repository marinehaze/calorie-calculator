01 Brand idea

Portion and proportion, not restriction.

Calm, not clinical

Warm cream ground, daylight photography, one berry accent. Data is present and exact, but it sits on a kitchen table rather than an instrument panel.

Proportion, not judgement

Protein, carbs and fat are shown as parts of a portion at equal weight. Nothing turns red at someone for eating. No good food, no bad food.

An honest estimate

Figures are database estimates against a portion you set. The open arc says so: precise enough to be useful, never claiming to be a measurement.

02 Naming · six candidates, three shortlisted, no winner yet

Six names. Three worth drawing.

Short, pronounceable, friendly, and pointing at the portion rather than the diet. “Calorie Calculator” stays as the product category line under any of them. Trademark screening has not been done at this stage.

Plated

Shortlisted

What is on the plate, already served. Food-positive, complete, calm. Prior use in the meal-kit category needs checking.

Piatto

Shortlisted

Italian for plate. Warm and easy to say, and the final “o” is a natural home for the arc.

Helping

Shortlisted

A helping is a portion, and the word is supportive by itself. The most human of the six.

Considered

Proportion itself. Clean wordmark, but it tips towards maths and clinical, and the name is crowded elsewhere.

Dollop

Considered

03 Wordmark · Piatto, Plus Jakarta Sans 600, tracked to −4.5% · the final “o” is the arc

The final “o” is the plate.

Lowercase, every letter the typeface’s own, and the final “o” redrawn as the partial arc: same width, same stem, round terminals, a 35° opening at one o’clock. The primary lockup carries no separate mark. In text and for assistive technology the name is always written “Piatto”.

04 App icon · cream, berry, one arc, no gradients

Three restrained icons.

The same geometry at three intensities. No numbers, no food, no illustration: the arc has to be recognisable at 29 px on a home screen, next to louder neighbours.

A · Rim

Canvas ground, berry arc centred at 62%. The most literal plate, and the calmest of the three.

B · Bleed

The arc enters from the bottom-right, the way the product’s photography enters a screen. More distinctive, slightly less legible when tiny.

C · Inverse

Berry ground, cream arc. Strongest on a crowded home screen; the least like the app’s own light surfaces.

05 The partial arc · one construction, every size

A plate from above. A portion of a whole.

One circle, one opening, round ends. The opening sits at one o’clock, where a portion is taken. It is never a gauge: there is no track behind it and it never fills towards a target.

As proportion

Proposed macro data colours

Today protein borrows berry, so the brand colour also means “protein”. This proposal separates brand from data: three food-adjacent hues that never carry an action, never mark a selection and never appear as a fill in the app. Marker plus word, always.
