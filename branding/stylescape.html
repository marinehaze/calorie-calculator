Stage 02 / Branding · Stylescape · Round 02

The same brand, held at two temperatures.

Round 01 read like an instrument panel: dark, monospaced, gridded. This round keeps what was working — the calm editorial structure, the light foundation, the belief that the number is the answer — and trades the engineering tone for something warmer and more contemporary. Both directions below are the same product: light, spacious, precise, with food visibly present. One is quiet. One speaks up. Neither is a dashboard.

Direction 01
Table

Warm paper, one deep accent, hairline rules. The dining table, not the data table.
Direction 02
Zest

Same light room, brand stepped forward. Marigold fields and a plate that counts.

The ground both directions stand on

Light foundation. A warm neutral paper, always. Dark is a mode the product can offer; it is never the brand.
Numbers in the brand’s own voice. Calories and macros are set in the display face with tabular figures — not handed to a monospace.
Monospace demoted. It survives at 10 px only, for units, sources and measurement labels. It is a detail, not an identity.
Food is present. Photographed, never illustrated, and never used as wallpaper behind text. It appears at full tonal quality or not at all.
One accent. Neutrals carry the structure; the accent only ever carries meaning.
Space before decoration. Margins are decided first. Nothing is added that a rule or a gap could do.
Shared placeholder name. Both use the wordmark Nota so personality is comparable. The name is a stand-in for expression, not a naming recommendation.

Deliberately left behind from Round 01

The dark instrument panel. It read clinical — a monitoring system rather than something you consult before lunch.
Monospace as the brand voice. Every figure in a developer typeface is what made the product feel like tooling.
Calibration ticks, rulers and dial gauges. Measurement theatre. The number is already precise; it does not need to be dressed as an instrument.
900-weight square grotesks. Confidence was coming from heaviness. It now comes from scale, colour and space.
The unit-count matrix. Memorable, but it reads as an analytics grid — the exact association to avoid.
Hairline data tables as primary layout. Kept only where a table is genuinely the right object; no longer the house style.
The hi-vis industrial accent. Replaced by colours that belong to food and to considered editorial work.
Direction 01 — Table Warm paper · Bramble · The marked rule

01

Table

The dining table, not the data table.

A calm, literate product that behaves like a well-edited reference you already trust. Warm paper, wide margins, one hairline rule system, and a deep berry accent spent only where a number matters. Nothing sits in a box: no cards, no containers, no chrome — everything rests directly on the paper.

Confidence here comes from restraint and scale. The calorie figure is the largest thing on the page because it is the answer, and the rest of the page is quiet enough to let it be. Food photography is generous and warm, cropped like a good cookbook rather than a menu.

Instrument Sans at 500, tracked in tight. The motif rule runs the full width of the word with a single accent tick beneath the o — the same mark that measures a value everywhere else in the system. Lowercase, no capitals, no symbol: the name behaves like a byline.

Two voices and a whisper. Instrument Sans carries anything that has to be remembered — the name, headings and every hero figure. DM Sans handles reading and secondary data. Monospace appears only at 10 px, and only on things that are literally measurements or sources.

Graphic motif — the marked ruleOne line, one tick

A hairline with a single mark on it

The whole system needs exactly one device, and this is it: a 1 px rule with a short accent tick at a meaningful position. It sets a heading, it measures a day, it marks the selected unit, it underlines the wordmark. Because the mark always means “this value, here”, it never becomes decoration — and it costs one pixel of ink.

Photography directionWarm, side-lit, generous

Tone & light study — stands in for photography, carries the grade not the subject

One soft source from the side, shadows allowed to fall deep and warm. Shot at eye level or three-quarters — never the flat-lay grid that reads as a menu. Ingredients stay in frame; hands are welcome; props and styling are not. Colour is warm and slightly desaturated, so the paper and the photograph belong to the same room.

Photography is used once per surface and never behind text. Where an image cannot be trusted to be good, the layout runs without one rather than filling the space.

Crop language

4:5 — the dish

3:2 — context & method

1:1 — list rows only

Three ratios, no others. Square is reserved for small list thumbnails so it never competes with a hero; 4:5 is the dish portrait; 3:2 is the only wide crop, used for method and context. Images are always flush to the text column, never inset in a card.

Direction 02 — Zest Light ground · Marigold · The plate

02

Zest

Same room. The brand walks in.

The identical light, spacious product — same margins, same belief that the number is the answer — but the brand stops whispering. A marigold field you can recognise across a room, a circular plate motif that crops the food, splits the macros and sits inside the wordmark, and figures set with real weight behind them.

The energy comes from scale and colour blocking, not from density. Corners stay soft, the type stays humanist, and the page still breathes — this is warmth turned up, not structure turned harder. It is the version people describe to someone else after using it once.

N ta

Manrope at 800, tracked tight, with the o replaced by the plate — split into the three macro arcs. The mark is the data visualisation at logo size, which means the identity and the information are literally the same object. Set solid in ink, it also works as a single-colour mark.

Three rules hold this together. Marigold carries area — it fills fields and buttons and never sets type. Ember carries emphasis — data marks, active states, one arc. Ink carries everything structural. The two warm hues are analogous, so the page reads as one temperature rather than as a palette, and the brightest thing on any screen after the marigold is the food itself.

Graphic motif — the plateOne circle, four jobs

A circle that is always the same circle

It crops the food, it splits into macros, it counts servings, and it stands in for the o in the wordmark. Four jobs, one shape — which is what makes it a brand device rather than a chart style. Round food imagery reads as a plate or a bowl without anyone having to say so, and it is the reason this direction can be bold without becoming hard-edged.

Photography directionBright, clean, saturated

Tone & light study — stands in for photography, carries the grade not the subject

Brighter and cleaner than Table: broad soft light, shallow shadows, colour left saturated. Shot from directly above when the dish is round, because the frame is going to become a plate. The food is the strongest colour on the page after the marigold — it is doing real work in the palette, not illustrating a paragraph.

Same discipline as Table on one point: an image is never a background. Where it meets the marigold field, it meets it edge to edge.

Where this stops. Branding exploration only. No application screens, no design system — type scales, spacing tokens, component states, motion and the full icon set are deliberately undefined and follow from whichever direction is chosen. Nota is a placeholder wordmark used purely to make personality comparable; it is not a naming proposal.

On the photography. Every image on this page is a tone and light study rather than a photograph — it carries the intended grade, contrast and crop, not the subject. Real imagery is the first thing to test once a direction is chosen.
